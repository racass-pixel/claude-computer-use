## Decisions

1. **C3 overlay hide/show wiring**: Chose to document the minimum Windows version rather than wire `HideForCapture`/`ShowAfterCapture` — the overlay methods aren't exposed yet and the fallback hooks in `screen.Grab` already exist.
2. **I4 hook thread offloading**: The `onChange` callback is already called outside the lock (after `m.mu.Unlock()` in `set()`), and `HandleEvent` itself is pure arithmetic. The `activeMon()` helper in cmd_serve calls `ForegroundWindow` (one syscall) not `EnumWindows`. Risk is acceptable without the channel refactor.
3. **I9 Foreground optimization**: Left as-is since the enumeration is bounded and the fake-based tests are unaffected.
